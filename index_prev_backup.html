DoCoG-PQA

20K preference pairs

Why DPO? SFT only imitates. Preferences teach the model to prefer a better chain — better answer, better reasoning, better grounding.

And it only works with an anchor

DPO is text-only — masks stay frozen. Without the anchor loss, grounding collapses: 81.9 → 41.7.
